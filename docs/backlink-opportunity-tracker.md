## Higher-value link opportunities to pursue next

1. Ask existing manufacturers, authorized distributors, and wholesale partners whether they maintain public **dealer/reseller or “where to buy” pages**. Request a listing only where Vonex is eligible and the details can be verified.
2. Offer an original, useful resource to Canadian small-business, office-management, education, and print-industry publishers—for example, a cartridge-number lookup guide or a plain-language OEM-vs-compatible toner explainer.
3. Look for genuine unlinked mentions of Vonex and ask the publisher to add the correct shop URL where it improves reader navigation.

## Reject

- Automated backlink generators, mass directory submissions, comment/forum spam, hidden links, link farms, private blog networks, scraped-content sites, and unrelated guest-post farms.
- Paid links intended to pass ranking credit, reciprocal-link schemes, or placements conditional on exact-match keyword anchors.
- Any listing that confuses the ecommerce store with local copier repair/leasing services, or claims a manufacturer authorization that cannot be verified.

## Prospect vetting checklist

For every prospect, record evidence—not just a third-party authority score:

1. Real editorial or business purpose and a relevant audience.
2. Canadian audience or clear relevance to Canadian printer-supply buyers.
3. Topical connection and a real reason for the link.
4. Eligibility, listing fees, editorial rules, and whether links are `nofollow`/`sponsored`.
5. Proposed target URL is canonical, live, useful, and indexable.
6. A genuine contribution or accurate company/product information to offer.
7. Contact owner, date, follow-up, response, and resulting live URL.

## Prioritization score (0–10)

- Topical relevance: 0–3
- Audience fit / Canadian reach: 0–2
- Editorial legitimacy and trust: 0–2
- Useful reason for the link: 0–2
- Feasibility / clear contact path: 0–1

**Suggested handling:** 8–10 = review first; 5–7 = research more; 0–4 = skip. This is a triage aid, not a prediction of ranking gains. Any spam/manipulation concern overrides the score and means reject.

## Measurement

Record a baseline before outreach and review monthly:
- Links earned and unique referring domains.
- Link relevance and target URL.
- Google Search Console clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position for the target page and relevant queries.
- Organic landing-page sessions, add-to-carts, checkout starts, and purchases.
- Outreach time and outcome.

Use Search Console and Shopify/analytics as first-party performance sources. Do not claim a ranking gain was caused by a single backlink without stronger evidence.
