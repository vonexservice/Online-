# Backlink Opportunity Tracker — shop.vonex.ca

**Status:** Research framework created; no backlink outreach or automated submissions have been performed.  
**Scope:** Canadian ecommerce SEO for `shop.vonex.ca`. Local service SEO for `www.vonex.ca` remains a separate campaign.

## Goal

Earn a small number of relevant, editorially legitimate mentions and links that help Canadian buyers discover useful Vonex printer-supply pages. A high backlink count is not the goal.

## Initial target page candidates

These are page *types*, not verified final URLs. Before outreach, confirm the canonical Shopify URL and that the page is live, useful, indexable, and relevant.

1. HP toner collection — commercial intent, Canada-wide
2. Brother toner collection — commercial intent, Canada-wide
3. Canon printer supplies/toner collection — commercial intent, Canada-wide
4. Printer ink and toner compatibility guide — informational intent
5. A genuinely useful printer-supplies buying guide — informational intent

Do not create new pages solely to attract links. Prefer strengthening existing pages first. Avoid directing all links to the homepage if a deeper page better answers the referring page's audience.

## Opportunity sources to research

- Existing supplier/manufacturer partner or dealer locator pages, but only where Vonex qualifies and the listing is accurate.
- Relevant Canadian business, procurement, and industry directories that screen listings and provide real user value.
- Canadian office-management, printing, education, healthcare administration, and small-business publications that accept genuinely useful expert contributions.
- Existing legitimate mentions of Vonex that do not link, and broken links to a resource Vonex can genuinely replace.
- Local Saskatchewan business associations only for the local-service domain and only when eligibility is real; do not use local citations as a substitute for national ecommerce links.
- Useful resource pages that reference printer maintenance, supplies, recycling, or purchasing guidance and are demonstrably relevant.

## Exclude

- Automated backlink generators, mass directory submission, comment/forum spam, hidden links, link farms, private blog networks, paid links that pass ranking credit, and exchanges designed primarily to manipulate rankings.
- Websites with no real audience or topical connection, scraped content, fake traffic claims, or unrelated guest-post farms.
- Any supplier/dealer claim, certification, discount, or product compatibility claim that Vonex cannot verify.
- Outreach that promises reciprocal links or asks for exact-match keyword anchors as a condition.

## Vetting checklist

For every prospect, record evidence—not just a third-party authority score:

1. Does the site have a real editorial or business purpose?
2. Is the audience in Canada or otherwise relevant to Canadian printer-supply buyers?
3. Is the proposed page topically related and useful to readers?
4. Is the page/indexing status and outbound-link context reasonable?
5. Is the placement editorially earned, clearly disclosed if sponsored, and compliant with the publisher's rules?
6. Is the proposed target URL canonical and live?
7. What unique information or resource can Vonex contribute?
8. Who owns outreach, what was sent, and what is the next follow-up date?

Do not automatically approve prospects using a Domain Rating/Domain Authority threshold. Those are third-party metrics, not Google ranking factors.

## Prioritization score (0–10)

- Topical relevance: 0–3
- Audience fit / Canadian reach: 0–2
- Editorial legitimacy and trust: 0–2
- Useful reason for the link: 0–2
- Feasibility / clear contact path: 0–1

**Suggested handling:** 8–10 = review first; 5–7 = research more; 0–4 = skip. This is a triage aid, not a prediction of ranking gains. Any spam/manipulation concern overrides the score and means reject.

## Measurement

Record baseline data before outreach and review monthly:
- Links actually earned and referring domains (deduplicate domains).
- Relevance and target URL for each link.
- Google Search Console clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position for the target page and relevant queries.
- Organic landing-page sessions and, where available, add-to-carts, checkout starts, and purchases.
- Outreach time and outcome.

Use Search Console and Shopify/analytics as first-party performance sources. Do not claim a ranking gain was caused by a single backlink without stronger evidence.

## First execution steps

1. Export the current GSC queries/pages for the exact `https://shop.vonex.ca/` property and confirm the canonical host.
2. Select the best 2–3 existing target pages from real query demand and conversion value.
3. Research prospects manually and add only evidence-backed candidates to `data/backlink-opportunities.csv`.
4. Review each prospect before any outreach; no automated link creation.
5. Record results and review after 30 days, then again after 60–90 days.
