## Higher-value opportunities to pursue

1. Ask existing manufacturers, authorized distributors, and wholesale partners whether they maintain public dealer/reseller or “where to buy” pages. Request a listing only where Vonex qualifies and details can be verified.
2. Offer useful content to Canadian small-business, office-management, education, and print-industry publishers—for example, an accurate cartridge-number lookup guide or an OEM-vs-compatible toner explainer.
3. Find genuine unlinked mentions of Vonex and ask publishers to link to the shop URL when it helps readers.
4. Consider directories only after checking editorial standards, actual audience, cost, and outbound-link attributes.

## Reject

- Automated backlink generators, mass directory submissions, comment/forum spam, hidden links, link farms, private blog networks, scraped-content sites, and unrelated guest-post farms.
- Paid links intended to pass ranking credit, reciprocal-link schemes, or placements conditional on exact-match keyword anchors.
- Any listing that confuses the ecommerce store with local copier repair/leasing services, or claims manufacturer authorization that cannot be verified.

## Prospect vetting checklist

1. Real editorial/business purpose and relevant audience.
2. Canadian audience or clear relevance to Canadian printer-supply buyers.
3. Topical connection and a genuine reason for the link.
4. Eligibility, listing fees, editorial rules, and whether links are `nofollow`/`sponsored`.
5. Proposed target URL is canonical, live, useful, and indexable.
6. A genuine contribution or accurate company/product information to offer.
7. Contact owner, date, follow-up, response, and resulting live URL.

## Prioritization score (0–10)

- Topical relevance: 0–3
- Audience fit / Canadian reach: 0–2
- Editorial legitimacy and trust: 0–2
- Useful reason for the link: 0–2
- Feasibility / clear contact path: 0–1

8–10 = review first; 5–7 = research more; 0–4 = skip. This is a triage aid, not a prediction of ranking gains. Any spam/manipulation concern means reject regardless of score.

## Measurement

Record monthly:
- Links earned and unique referring domains.
- Link relevance and target URL.
- GSC clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position for each target page and query.
- Organic landing sessions, add-to-carts, checkout starts, and purchases.
- Outreach time and outcome.

Review after 30 days and again after 60–90 days. Do not claim a ranking gain was caused by a single backlink without stronger evidence.
