# Vonex SEO Growth Engine

Purpose: central operating system for SEO research and execution across:

- https://www.vonex.ca — local/service authority
- https://shop.vonex.ca — Canadian ecommerce authority

## Operating model

1. Pull first-party performance from Google Search Console, Shopify and analytics.
2. Separate indexing/crawl health from ranking/content work.
3. Research competitor rankings, keywords, backlinks, and content gaps.
4. Prioritize opportunities where Vonex can realistically gain visibility and revenue.
5. Map every keyword to one canonical Vonex URL.
6. Improve existing pages/products before creating unnecessary new URLs.
7. Build useful supporting content and legitimate citations/mentions.
8. Track indexing, clicks, CTR, rankings, conversions, product coverage, and referring domains.
9. Review weekly and adjust priorities.

### Indexing rule

**Do not chase an index count. Chase the right index.**

Index:
- strong collection/category pages with real search demand
- useful brand/model/cartridge pages
- high-quality products with unique titles, descriptions, compatibility data and inventory
- important evergreen informational pages

Do not index:
- internal search results
- filter/facet combinations with no standalone search intent
- duplicate supplier/import URLs
- thin or placeholder pages
- expired/discontinued products without a useful replacement
- utility, account, cart, checkout and other non-search pages

Use canonicalization, redirects and noindex deliberately. Do not use robots.txt as the main method for removing already-discovered URLs from Google's index.

## Weekly target

Target 10–20 meaningful SEO actions/assets/opportunities per week, not 10–20 manufactured backlinks.

Every action should be tied to:
- keyword/search intent
- canonical target URL
- indexing decision
- business value
- measurable outcome

## Guardrails

We do not mass-generate backlinks or thin Google properties. Google considers automated link creation intended to manipulate rankings link spam. Supporting assets must have a real user purpose and add useful information.

We also do not mass-publish thin product copy merely to increase indexed URL counts. Product pages should earn indexation through useful, differentiated information.

## Success metrics

Primary:
- number and percentage of wanted URLs indexed
- reduction in unwanted/duplicate URLs
- GSC clicks and impressions
- non-brand commercial queries entering page 1
- organic revenue/conversions

Secondary:
- CTR
- referring domains and relevant citations
- collection/product engagement
- Merchant Center approval/coverage
- crawl/indexing errors
